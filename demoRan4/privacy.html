Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At ViralVibe AI, we collect information to provide a better experience with our AI video idea generation tools. This includes:

  • Account Details: Email addresses and profiles created to access our dashboards and schedule posts.
  • Content Inputs: Niche topics, platforms, tones, and text prompts specified in the generator control panel.
  • Interaction Data: Clicking behavior, selected platform categories, and metrics related to generated ideas.

2. How We Use Information

We use the data we collect to operate, build, and support the ViralVibe service, specifically to:

  • Deliver mockups, preview templates, and social media posting metrics.
  • Refine and train our predictive algorithms regarding social media performance.
  • Answer inquiries, support contact requests, and distribute product updates.

3. Data Security & Storage

We take security seriously and enforce multiple layout controls to protect your data. All communication is encrypted via TLS. While we employ top-tier safeguards, no transmission channel is completely secure; therefore, we recommend maintaining strong password practices.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please send an inquiry through our Contact Page or email us at privacy@viralvibe.ai.
