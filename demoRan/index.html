Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Welcome to ViralLoop AI. We take your privacy very seriously and are dedicated to maintaining the trust of our users. This privacy policy explains how our application functions with respect to your accounts and content.

1. Data Collection & Simulation

Currently, this website operates as a functional technical demonstration workspace. Any inputs you enter, including video niches, target audiences, and prompts, are processed strictly inside your browser environment to generate simulated responses. We do not store or transmit this data to external database servers.

2. Social Media Accounts

When you eventually connect social media channels to our API, we only request permissions necessary to queue, preview, and post contents at your selected intervals. We do not inspect messages or external metrics outside of your publication data.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We may use local storage to temporarily save the posts you schedule during this mock demo session, allowing you to preview the scheduling calendar. This data can be cleared at any time by refreshing or clearing browser data.

4. Updates to This Policy

We will occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Users will be notified of changes via layout announcements on our homepage.
