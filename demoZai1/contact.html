Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and return policies.

Are all Zai Essentials products organic? ▼
Yes, all products in our Health and Beauty categories are USDA organic certified or made with 100% naturally derived botanical ingredients. Our Essentials catalog features sustainably sourced, chemical-free home goods.
Do you ship internationally? ▼
We currently ship to the United States, Canada, and select European countries. Free shipping is automatically applied to all domestic orders over $50.
What is your return policy? ▼
We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on all essentials. If a skincare or health product doesn't agree with you, simply reach out to hello@zaiessentials.com for a prepaid return label and full refund.
Are the bottles recyclable? ▼
Yes! All our wellness and beauty products are housed in premium glass apothecary bottles that can be recycled or repurposed. We offer refill pouches for select products to reduce waste.
