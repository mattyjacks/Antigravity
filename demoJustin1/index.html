Meet Chef Justin Singson

Growing up in the bustling markets of Manila, Justin was always fascinated by the simple comfort of street food. After training in Michelin-starred kitchens in Melbourne and Paris, he returned to the Philippines with a vision: to elevate local street favorites to the high-end dining table.

"The hotdog is the ultimate canvas of flavor. By marrying it with culinary techniques and the rich ingredients of our provinces, we create a sensory experience that feels both nostalgic and groundbreaking."

Singson Hotdogs is the realization of that dream—where fine dining craftsmanship meets the unpretentious joy of hotdogs.
