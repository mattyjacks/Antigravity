Saima's Journey

Born and raised in Pakistan, Saima began her programming journey in high school. Her fascination with automation, software architecture, and the power of local communities led her to study Computer Science and ultimately launch a career in software engineering.

Seeing the disparity in localized tech education, she decided to record her first coding tutorial. What started as a hobby quickly turned into a movement, growing to over 250,000 developers across YouTube, LinkedIn, and Instagram.
