Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Saima.Tech ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose personal data when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, or submit inquiries through our partnership forms.

This policy is designed to comply with United States federal and state laws, specifically aligning with the New Hampshire Privacy Act (NHPA / SB 255).

1. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identifiers: Your name, business name, and email address when you voluntarily sign up for our newsletter or submit a collaboration inquiry.
  • Internet or Network Activity: IP addresses, browser types, browser versions, and details on how you navigate our site via cookies and basic web analytics.

2. How We Use Your Personal Data

We process your personal data for the following specific purposes:

  • To deliver our weekly newsletters and tech tutorial updates.
  • To evaluate, respond to, and fulfill brand collaboration and sponsorship requests.
  • To maintain, optimize, and secure the performance of our website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your personal data for profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Disclosures and Sales of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We also do not share your personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. We only share personal data with trusted third-party service providers (such as email delivery hosting companies and analytics tools) who assist us in operating our platform.

4. Your Rights Under the New Hampshire Privacy Act (NHPA)

If you are a resident of New Hampshire, you are entitled to exercise the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Confirm and Access: Confirm whether we are processing your personal data and access the specific pieces of data collected.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccuracies in the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Portability: Obtain a portable, readily usable copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of any processing for targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or profiling. (Note: We do not engage in these practices).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you (e.g., charge different rates or deny service) for exercising any of your privacy rights.

5. Submitting a Rights Request & Appeal Process

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a request to us at matt@mattyjacks.com. We will respond to your request within 45 days.

If we decline to take action on your request, you have the right to appeal our decision within 30 days of the denial. You can submit your appeal via email to matt@mattyjacks.com. If the appeal is denied, you may contact the New Hampshire Attorney General to lodge a complaint.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can remove the information.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our compliance practices, please contact us at:

Email: matt@mattyjacks.com
