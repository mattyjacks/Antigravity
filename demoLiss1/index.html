Domestic & Street Cats of Ecuador: Coexistence & Protection

In the colonial plazas of Quito, Cuenca, and coastal Guayaquil, domestic felines (*Felis catus*) share human history. However, unmanaged feral cat populations pose an existential threat to native birdlife, reptiles, and endemic Galápagos species. Ecuador's leading conservation biosecurity projects prioritize comprehensive trap-neuter-return (TNR), indoor feline enrichment, and domestic cat containment to protect wild ecosystems.

  • 🛡️ Galápagos Biosecurity: Strictly regulated domestic animal quarantines prevent invasive diseases like feline leukemia from jumping to Galápagos sea lions or birds.
  • 🏡 "Catio" & Indoor Enrichment: Keeping domestic cats indoors protects urban wildlife and keeps pets safe from high Andes traffic and urban parasites.
  • 🩺 Community Spay/Neuter: Grassroots programs in Pichincha and Azuay foster compassionate coexistence while defending wild felid genetic territories.
