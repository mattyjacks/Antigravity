THE PROVENANCE

Fifteen Years of Relentless Pursuit

Founded by Wally Stack, It's Wally's World originated from a deep-seated passion for unearthing forgotten American pop culture treasures from estate liquidations and storage unit auctions across New York.

"Every comic slab, vintage jacket, and diecast model has a story. Our mission is preserving that history and placing it into the hands of passionate collectors."

Today, what began as a tireless quest has evolved into two premier Long Island hubs housing over 25,000 carefully curated nostalgic artifacts, uniting vintage lovers, comic historians, and grail hunters worldwide.
